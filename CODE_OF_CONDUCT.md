# Code of conduct

LeonidaReforge welcomes contributors with different backgrounds and levels of experience. This policy applies
to the project's issues, pull requests, reviews, and other spaces the organization administers.

## Working together

- Treat people with respect. Critique the proposal or evidence, not the person. Explain review requests and
  make room for questions, corrections, and differences of opinion.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, stalk, impersonate, or publish another person's private information.
- Respect contributor attribution and the project's [rules on redistribution](docs/redistribution-review.md).
  Do not post proprietary game material, credentials, or private security reports.
- Do not retaliate against someone for raising a concern in good faith.

## Reporting and enforcement

Ask an uninvolved maintainer for a private reporting channel. Do not post a sensitive incident in an issue
or pull request. If you cannot reach an uninvolved maintainer, or a report concerns the only available
maintainer, use [GitHub's abuse reporting process](https://support.github.com/contact/report-abuse).
For software vulnerabilities, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Maintainers not involved in the incident should review the available evidence, limit disclosure to those who
need it, and choose a proportionate response. Responses can include a warning, removal of harmful content, or
restrictions on participation. An affected person can request reconsideration by an uninvolved maintainer.
No response or investigation outcome is guaranteed within a fixed time.
